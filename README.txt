# Hariom Enterprises Website

A free, responsive static business website for Hariom Enterprises - Iron & Steel Products Supplier.

## Included
- Responsive homepage
- Products section
- About Us
- Why Choose Us
- Contact/enquiry form
- WhatsApp enquiry button
- No paid software required

## Before publishing
Open `index.html` and replace:
- +91 XXXXX XXXXX
- 91XXXXXXXXXX (both phone/WhatsApp references)
- info@hariomenterprises.in
- Your business address

Also update the `whatsappNumber` in `script.js`.

## Publish for free
You can upload these three files (`index.html`, `style.css`, `script.js`) to a free static hosting service such as GitHub Pages or Netlify. A custom domain is optional and normally costs extra.
